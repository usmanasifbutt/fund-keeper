# Privacy

_Last updated: 13 October 2026_

Keeper is built so that your financial data stays with you.

## What Keeper stores

All of this is stored **only in your phone's browser storage** (localStorage):

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your holdings: fund, units, amount invested, optional first-invested date, card order | To work out your profit/loss |
| A daily total of your portfolio's value and amount invested | To draw the profit/loss chart |
| The last copy of MUFAP's public prices | So the app opens instantly and works offline |

## What Keeper does not do

- No accounts, sign-ups or logins.
- No server of its own. Your holdings are never uploaded anywhere.
- No analytics, tracking, cookies or ads.
- It doesn't ask for your bank, fund company or brokerage logins.

## Network requests

The app makes only two kinds of requests:

1. **The app itself**, from GitHub Pages (or wherever you host it). GitHub may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type; see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
2. **MUFAP's public Performance Summary page**, to get prices. This request contains no information about your holdings. MUFAP may log standard request details, as any website does.

## Backups

**Save backup file** creates a `.json` file on your device containing your holdings. Where you keep it (Drive, email, etc.) is up to you, and that service's privacy terms apply.

## Deleting your data

Clear the site data for Keeper in your browser settings, or uninstall the home screen app and clear the browser's data. This permanently removes your holdings from the phone, so save a backup first if you want to keep them.
